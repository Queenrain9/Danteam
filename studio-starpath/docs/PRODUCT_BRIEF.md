# Product brief / Research and concept · 2026-09-25

User constraints: mobile; Korea and Japan. Creative and production decisions are delegated. Commercial-ready quality is the destination, **not** a label attached to the first playable build. Preserve reversibility of early creative decisions.

Evidence: Sensor Tower Japan 2026 reports >US$10B in Japanese mobile IAP revenue for the 12 months ending July 2026 and describes puzzle leading downloads while RPG and strategy lead revenue (https://sensortower.com/report/state-of-gaming-in-japan-2026). The 2025 Japan market report similarly observes casual-puzzle download strength, but does not demonstrate that a new original puzzle will sell (https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-japan-gaming-2025). South Korea's puzzle chart contains accessible puzzles alongside paid authored puzzle games; chart listings are **not** preference studies for the whole country (https://app.sensortower.com/top-charts?category=7012&country=KR&device=iphone&os=ios). Hunicke/LeBlanc/Zubek's MDA framework distinguishes authored mechanics, emergent dynamics and actual player experience (https://aaai.org/papers/ws04-04-001-mda-a-formal-approach-to-game-design-and-game-research/).

Experimental concept: tactile path-planning puzzle with an expressive original letter-carrying companion. The player selects routes, optional snack detours and gate/seal logic. The companion remembers **actual** route choices and the room evolves from progress. Shared small expeditions are the intended experience, not a click-to-fill-affection meter. No punitive offline timers, gambling, ads, monetization, network accounts or forced daily rewards in P1.

Alternatives intentionally kept open: expressive pet world-building; short tactile sound-light puzzles. P1 tests the route-courier hook first, but no claim of a verified market winner. Uncertainty: puzzle completion could overshadow companion attachment; an algorithmically solvable puzzle is not necessarily an enjoyable human experience. Human playtests and commercial validation remain open gates.

Stage gates: P0 research and reversible concept; P1 12-stage executable slice, bilingual mobile UI, domain solvability tests and technical QA; P2 consenting unassisted Korean/Japanese external playtesting and concept comparison; P3 system/content/art expansion, quality and native-device matrix, localization review, licensing, privacy/store compliance, commercial release candidate and owner authorization. NO release claim before P2/P3 evidence.
